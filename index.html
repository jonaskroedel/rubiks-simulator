Standard Rubik's Cube Notation Guide

Basic Face Turns (Clockwise 90°)

  • U — Up (Top) face clockwise
  • D — Down (Bottom) face clockwise
  • L — Left face clockwise
  • R — Right face clockwise
  • F — Front face clockwise
  • B — Back face clockwise

Move Modifiers

  • ' (Prime) — Turn counter-clockwise (e.g. R', U')
  • 2 — Double turn 180° (e.g. R2, U2)
  • 2' — Double turn in reverse direction

Wide Moves (2 Layers)

  • Rw or r — Turn Right face and adjacent inner layer
  • Lw or l — Turn Left face and adjacent inner layer
  • Uw or u — Turn Top 2 layers
  • Dw or d — Turn Bottom 2 layers
  • Fw or f — Turn Front 2 layers
  • Bw or b — Turn Back 2 layers

Slice Moves (3x3 & 4x4)

  • M (Middle) — Slice between L and R, turns in direction of L
  • E (Equator) — Slice between U and D, turns in direction of D
  • S (Standing) — Slice between F and B, turns in direction of F
  • 2R, 2U (4x4) — Turn single 2nd inner slice

Whole Cube Rotations

  • x — Rotate entire cube in direction of R (tilt up)
  • y — Rotate entire cube in direction of U (turn left)
  • z — Rotate entire cube in direction of F (roll clockwise)
